Customer Reviews

Vladimir N.

Tasty and filling, with a good amount of sauce; the fries were well-salted and the portion size was excellent. It was a bit heavy on the pepper—quite spicy—though likely perfect for fans of very hot food. The order arrived quickly and was still piping hot.

Alikhan P.

It’s fantastic - they reply to messages instantly, orders are ready really fast, and the burgers are delicious and juicy - exactly what you want. The fries with the signature sauce are amazing. The Spicy Crispy Burger is a real "wow" and a must-try. The Seoul Burger is packed with sauce - it even overflowed a bit for me—but it’s great. The Teriyaki Burger wasn't quite my thing; it’s a matter of personal taste. The location is convenient, and the interior is spacious with plenty of tables. The staff are lovely - always calm and friendly, with no long waits—and the music is great, too.

Amina R.

A really lovely place! 🥢 I’d like to give a special mention to the service—the staff are polite, attentive, and genuinely care about their guests. Everything is handled quickly, neatly, and with a smile, yet without being intrusive. They take orders carefully, explain everything, and help with choices. The atmosphere is cozy, the food is delicious, and the excellent service makes you want to come back again. Highly recommended! 🔥
