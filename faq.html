Frequently Asked Questions

Question 1: How long does delivery take, and how do you preserve the freshness of the rolls?

We never work with pre-made rolls — our chefs begin crafting your order only after it is confirmed. Average delivery time takes 35 to 50 minutes, depending on your location. To maintain top restaurant-quality taste, we use premium sealed eco-packaging and specialized thermal bags: cold rolls with fresh salmon and tuna stay properly chilled, while baked and tempura rolls arrive hot and crispy.

Question 2: What are the delivery terms, and is there a free delivery threshold?

All orders are delivered by our own dedicated courier service. We offer free delivery on all orders starting from 7,000 ₸. For orders below this amount, the delivery fee is calculated automatically based on your zone during checkout. You can also choose the Self-Pickup option from our restaurant to receive an exclusive 10% discount on your entire order.

Question 3: What comes included with my order, and can I add special delivery instructions?

Every order includes complimentary premium soy sauce, signature wasabi, white pickled ginger, and high-quality bamboo chopsticks based on the number of guests you specify (trainer chopstick holders are available upon request). During checkout, you can also opt for contactless delivery or leave custom instructions for the courier (such as “Please don't ring the doorbell, baby is sleeping” or “Leave with the concierge”), and our team will follow them precisely.
